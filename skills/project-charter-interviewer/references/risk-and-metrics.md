# Risks And Metrics

## Risk Register

Assess each risk by likelihood and impact. Record the first observable warning signal, a specific mitigation, and an owner. Escalate high-impact risks with no owner or no mitigation.

Common categories:

- Scope: unclear acceptance criteria, expansion requests, missing exclusions.
- Dependency: unavailable input, vendor, system, approval, legal review, or stakeholder.
- Delivery: capability gap, capacity gap, brittle process, or fixed deadline.
- Quality: inaccurate data, weak validation, inaccessible output, or inconsistent execution.
- Adoption: wrong audience, insufficient distribution, change resistance, or unsupported workflow.
- Compliance: privacy, intellectual property, platform policy, or brand restrictions.

## Metric Design

Write a metric as: **change in [measure] for [audience] from [baseline] to [target] during [window], measured by [source].**

Use leading indicators to show whether execution is on track and lagging indicators to judge the final result. Do not use a vanity metric unless it is tied to a stated objective.

Examples:

- Increase qualified landing-page sign-ups from 3.2% to 4.0% within four weeks, measured in product analytics.
- Reduce average manual asset-preparation time from 45 minutes to 15 minutes per request during the pilot, measured from work logs.
- Reach 80% task-completion success among first-time users in moderated usability testing.
