# AI视频运镜Prompt示例库

适用平台：Sora / Kling(可灵) / Runway / Pika / Hailuo

---

## 一、基础运镜Prompt

### 固定镜头
```
Static shot, medium close-up, a young woman speaking directly to camera 
in a minimalist white room, soft diffused lighting, shallow depth of field, 
4K cinematic
```

### 推镜头
```
Slow dolly in from medium shot to close-up, a man sitting at a wooden desk 
writing in a journal, warm golden hour light streaming through window, 
smooth and steady movement
```

### 拉镜头
```
Dolly out from extreme close-up of an eye to wide shot revealing a person 
standing on a cliff overlooking the ocean, dramatic sunset lighting, 
cinematic widescreen
```

### 横移
```
Smooth lateral tracking shot moving left to right, revealing a row of 
colorful storefronts on a European cobblestone street, morning light, 
medium shot, steady pace
```

### 跟随
```
Camera follows from behind as a woman walks through a bustling Tokyo 
night market, medium shot, neon lights reflecting on wet pavement, 
handheld feel with gimbal stabilization
```

### 环绕
```
Slow 180-degree orbit around a dancer frozen in pose, studio lighting 
with dramatic rim light, full shot, smooth circular movement, 
black background
```

### 升降
```
Crane shot rising from street level up to rooftop, revealing the entire 
city skyline at golden hour, starting from close-up of flowers on a 
balcony to extreme wide shot of the metropolis
```

### 摇镜头
```
Slow pan right from an empty chair to reveal a woman standing by the 
window, medium shot, soft natural light, interior of a vintage apartment, 
gentle and deliberate movement
```

### 甩镜头
```
Whip pan transition from a person throwing a ball to another person 
catching it in a different location, fast motion blur connecting two 
scenes, bright daylight, energetic mood
```

### 晃动/手持
```
Handheld close-up following a chef's hands as they plate a dish, 
subtle natural shake, warm kitchen lighting with steam rising, 
documentary style, shallow depth of field
```

## 二、口播场景Prompt

### 场景1：正面直拍口播
```
Static shot, medium close-up, a confident young woman speaking to camera, 
modern office background with blurred bookshelves, ring light reflection 
in eyes, shallow depth of field, clean and professional look
```

### 场景3：走路口播
```
Tracking shot following a man walking forward on a tree-lined sidewalk, 
camera moves backward facing the subject, medium shot, autumn afternoon 
golden light filtering through leaves, steady gimbal movement
```

### 场景5：车内口播
```
Static close-up shot inside a car, dashboard camera angle, a person 
speaking while occasionally glancing at the road, natural daylight 
through windshield, subtle car vibration
```

### 场景7：探店口播
```
Handheld tracking shot following a woman entering a cozy coffee shop, 
pushing through the door, camera pans to reveal the interior, warm 
ambient lighting, medium shot transitioning to wide shot, 
vlog documentary style
```

### 场景9：产品展示口播
```
Start with medium shot of a person holding a product, slow dolly in 
to extreme close-up of the product details, then pull back to medium 
close-up of the person explaining, soft studio lighting, 
clean white background
```

## 三、复合运镜Prompt

### 推 + 升
```
Camera slowly dollies in while craning upward, starting from a low-angle 
medium shot of a warrior to a dramatic high-angle close-up against 
stormy clouds, epic cinematic lighting, slow and powerful movement
```

### 希区柯克变焦
```
Dolly zoom effect on a person standing in a long corridor, background 
stretches while subject stays same size, creating vertigo sensation, 
fluorescent overhead lighting, unsettling mood
```

### 航拍揭示
```
Drone shot starting close to morning mist on a lake surface, ascending 
rapidly to reveal an entire mountain landscape with a mirror-like lake, 
golden sunrise light, breathtaking aerial reveal, 
extreme wide shot at peak
```

### FPV穿越
```
FPV drone shot racing through an abandoned warehouse, weaving between 
pillars and through broken windows, emerging into bright daylight 
outside, fast-paced continuous shot, industrial atmosphere
```
