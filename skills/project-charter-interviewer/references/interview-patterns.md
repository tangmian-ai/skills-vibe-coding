# Interview Patterns

## Creative Campaign Or Brand Work

Clarify the audience insight, communication objective, single-minded message, channels, markets, formats, production constraints, brand guardrails, approval route, usage rights, and measurement method. Separate a visual direction from a business objective.

## Product Or Feature Work

Clarify the user problem, target segment, current journey, desired behavior, release boundary, technical dependencies, instrumentation, rollout plan, support impact, and decision owner. Ask for a baseline before accepting an uplift target.

## Operational Workflow Or Automation

Clarify the present process, trigger, inputs, transformation steps, outputs, exception paths, human review points, permissions, audit requirements, handoff owners, and time or quality cost being reduced.

## Research Or Discovery Work

Clarify the decision the research will inform, hypotheses, target participants or evidence sources, method, sample constraints, deliverable format, confidence threshold, and decision meeting date.

## Question Selection

- Prefer questions that resolve a decision, scope boundary, owner, or measurement gap.
- Avoid asking for detail before agreeing on the outcome and minimum viable scope.
- Translate subjective phrases such as “premium,” “viral,” or “easy to use” into observable criteria.
- For every new request, ask whether it changes scope, timing, quality, cost, or risk.
