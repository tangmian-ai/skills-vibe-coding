# Brand E-commerce Creative

Use this skill after a brand direction and required copy are known. It adapts one approved creative proposition; it does not invent a new brand direction for every channel.

## Workflow

1. Lock audience, market, brand kit, product facts, approved copy, and mandatory assets.
2. Select the required channel specification: social, domestic e-commerce, cross-border e-commerce, livestream, private-domain, print, or KV extension.
3. Translate the parent direction into each ratio while retaining hierarchy, visual focal point, product truth, and legibility.
4. Generate or compose per placement. Do not treat a simple crop as channel adaptation.
5. Verify copy, safe zones, logo use, product claims, localization, and thumbnail-scale readability.
6. Package files with channel, dimensions, language/market, copy version, asset version, and QA status.

## Output

Provide a placement plan, per-channel brief, visual prompt or production spec, and a delivery manifest.

Use the source references in `references/` for brand-kit, social, e-commerce, livestream, print, and KV requirements.
